# GDPR, CCPA and personal data

- Under **GDPR** we act as a **processor**; you are the controller.
- Under **CCPA** we act as a **service provider**.
- The app uses Atlassian's **Personal Data Reporting** API: it reports the account IDs it stores at least every seven days and erases or refreshes data for closed or changed accounts.
- Data subject requests: contact info@berkly.nl. Full erasure is also done by uninstalling.
