# Performance

Are we keeping our SLAs? Performance counts **finished** timers only, so running timers don't skew the figures.

- Pick a **Period** (**7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **Custom**) and **Group by** (**SLA**, **Metric**, **Priority**, **Assignee** or **Work type**).
- Tiles: **On time** (share of finished timers that met their target, with the change against the previous period), **Met**, **Missed** and **Typical time used** (median of time used ÷ target).
- A chart of on time by day (by week for periods over 30 days), and a table per group: On time, Met, Missed, Avg time, Reopened.
- **Missed work items (n)** lists each miss with its metric and the date it was missed. It loads when you open it.

> **Note:** The old **Report** tab is part of this page now: **Group by → Metric** is its table, and **Missed work items** its list.
