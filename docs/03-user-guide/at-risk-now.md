# Needs attention

What to work on next: timers that are breached or due soon, most overdue first.

- The strip at the top counts the project's **Breached**, **At risk**, **On track** and **Paused** timers. Select a count to show only those rows.
- **Due within** (**Today**, **3 days**, **This week**, **Any time**) and the **SLA**, **Assignee** and **Priority** filters narrow the list.
- **Time left** reads *3h 20m left* or *Overdue 2d 4h* and counts down on screen. Outside working hours it says when the timer resumes.
- **Due** is on your own clock. When the working hours use another timezone, the ⓘ beside it gives their time.
- **Open in Jira search** shows the breached timers in Jira search.
